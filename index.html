THE BASICS

Lymphoma, simplified.

Start with the system that helps your body defend itself.

YOUR LYMPHATIC SYSTEM

Part of your immune defenses

Lymph nodes and lymphatic vessels are parts of a network that supports immune function and fluid balance.

What is lymphoma?

Lymphoma is a group of cancers that begin in lymphocytes, a type of white blood cell involved in the immune system.

Hodgkin lymphoma

A distinct lymphoma group with characteristic diagnostic features.

Non-Hodgkin lymphomas

A broad group of different lymphomas with varied features and treatments.

AWARENESS, NOT ALARM

Know the signs.
Don't jump to conclusions.

Possible signs can have many causes. A healthcare professional can help assess what they mean.

○

Enlarged lymph nodes

A lump or swelling, sometimes painless, such as in the neck, armpit, or groin.

☼

Unexplained fever

Fever without an obvious cause that deserves medical assessment if persistent.

≈

Drenching night sweats

Sweating heavily at night, not simply feeling warm under bedding.

↘

Unintentional weight loss

Noticeable weight loss without trying, especially alongside other persistent symptoms.

♡

Persistent fatigue

Ongoing tiredness that does not improve as expected and affects daily life.

Important to remember

These symptoms are not proof of lymphoma. Many common conditions can cause them. Seek medical advice for persistent, unexplained, or concerning symptoms.

UNDERSTANDING LYMPHOMA

Your body has a story.
Let's understand it.

Your lymphatic system helps protect your body. Learn what happens when lymphocytes grow abnormally, which signs deserve attention, and how doctors investigate lymphoma.

Knowledge, not fear

Many symptoms associated with lymphoma can also occur in other conditions. This website provides general education, not a diagnosis.

01 Meet your lymphatic system +
The lymphatic system is a network of vessels, lymph nodes, and organs that helps maintain fluid balance and supports immune defence. Lymph nodes filter lymph and contain immune cells. They can enlarge for many reasons, including common infections.
02 What is lymphoma? +
Lymphoma is a group of cancers that begin in lymphocytes, a type of white blood cell. The two main categories are Hodgkin lymphoma and non-Hodgkin lymphoma. They include different diseases, so their evaluation and treatment can vary.
03 Know the signs +
Possible signs include a persistent or unexplained swollen lymph node, unexplained fever, drenching night sweats, unintentional weight loss, or ongoing fatigue. These symptoms can have many causes and do not automatically mean lymphoma. Seek medical advice for persistent, worsening, or concerning symptoms.
04 From questions to answers +
A clinician may ask about symptoms and medical history and perform an examination. Depending on the situation, tests may include blood tests, imaging, and a lymph node biopsy. A biopsy is often essential to identify the type of lymphoma. The tests needed depend on each person's circumstances.

LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

Myth or fact?

Tap a statement to reveal a short explanation.

Every swollen lymph node is lymphoma.

Myth. Swollen lymph nodes often occur with infections and other conditions. Persistent or concerning swelling should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

A biopsy may be needed to confirm lymphoma.

Fact. Examination of tissue is often important to confirm the diagnosis and identify the lymphoma type.

Symptoms alone can tell you which lymphoma you have.

Myth. Symptoms can guide evaluation, but diagnosis and classification require appropriate medical investigations.
